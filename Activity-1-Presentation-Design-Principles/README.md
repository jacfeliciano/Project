📌 Overview

Activity 1 focused on applying basic presentation and graphic design principles to create a promotional coffee poster. The goal was to organize the information in a way that was visually appealing while making the important details easy for the audience to notice and understand.

The design was created around a coffee-shop advertisement, combining typography, imagery, color, and layout to create a welcoming and modern café atmosphere.

💡 Concept

The concept of the poster was centered on promoting Renberio Coffee and creating the feeling of a casual and inviting coffee-shop experience. A café background was used as the main visual setting, while the darker overlay helped the text and other elements stand out.

The design also incorporated a person holding a coffee cup as a major visual element, helping reinforce the main theme of the advertisement. The combination of neon-style text, bold lettering, and the café setting was used to create a youthful and energetic appearance.

🎨 Design Principles Applied
1. Visual Hierarchy

Different font sizes and styles were used to guide the viewer's attention. The large "RENBERIO COFFEE" text serves as the main title, while the smaller text provides supporting information such as the date, location, and promotional message.

2. Contrast

Contrast was created through the use of bright white and glowing yellow text against the darker café background. This makes the important information more noticeable and prevents the text from blending into the background.

3. Alignment

The text and other elements were positioned carefully within the poster's frame to maintain an organized composition. The information at the lower portion of the poster was also grouped together to make the contact and location details easier to read.

4. Balance

The visual elements were distributed across the poster to create balance. The large title occupies the upper and central area, while the person holding the coffee cup provides a strong visual element on the right side. This helps balance the amount of text on the left and center portions of the design.

5. Consistency

A consistent visual style was maintained through the use of a dark café background, white typography, glowing yellow accents, and a rounded rectangular border. These elements work together to give the poster a unified appearance.

6. Simplicity

The background was darkened so it would not compete too strongly with the foreground elements. This allowed the title, promotional message, and main image to remain the primary focus while still keeping the café environment visible.

🛠️ Development Process
Chose a coffee-shop promotional concept for the poster.
Selected a café background that matched the theme.
Adjusted the background to create a darker and less distracting setting.
Added the main Renberio Coffee title using bold typography.
Incorporated neon-style text to create a coffee-shop atmosphere.
Added the main visual of a person holding a coffee cup.
Organized the promotional details, date, location, and contact information.
Added a rounded border to frame the overall composition.
Adjusted the positioning, spacing, and sizing of the elements.
Reviewed the final design to improve readability and visual balance.

✅ Final Output

The final output is a promotional coffee poster that combines typography, imagery, contrast, and layout to communicate information in a visually engaging way. The dark café background establishes the setting, while the bright typography and main image draw attention to the advertisement.

Overall, the design demonstrates how presentation principles can be applied to create a poster that is both informative and visually appealing, while maintaining a clear focus on the coffee-shop theme.
