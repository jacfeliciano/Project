# Activity 4 – Google Meet and Google Forms
Activity Description

This activity explores the purpose, features, and practical applications of Google Meet and Google Forms as digital tools for communication, collaboration, and information management.

What Have I Learned?

Through this activity, I learned how Google Meet can be used for online meetings, virtual discussions, and collaboration. I also learned how Google Forms helps users create surveys, quizzes, and online forms to collect and organize information efficiently. One challenge was understanding the different features and applications of both platforms, which I addressed by exploring their tools and functions. I can apply these skills in future academic activities, group projects, and online collaborations to communicate and gather information more effectively.
