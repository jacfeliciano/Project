# Activity 7
This activity involved using two large language models, ChatGPT and Gemini, to generate a tailored, one-page professional resume for a Nursing Virtual Assistant position using an identical prompt. I then evaluated both outputs across four criteria Structure, Clarity, Formatting, and Overall Quality to synthesize a polished final resume.

What have I learned?

Key Concepts and Skills Gained: I gained practical skills in prompt engineering, critical evaluation of AI-generated content, and tailoring clinical nursing experiences into transferable remote-healthcare competencies such as digital patient documentation, medical terminology, and patient confidentiality.

Challenge Faced and Solution: One challenge was that each AI model prioritized different strengths—Gemini created a well-balanced, full-page layout with strong action verbs, while ChatGPT maintained stricter factual accuracy regarding my expected 2027 graduation timeline and supervised clinical status. I resolved this by comparing both drafts side by side and combining Gemini’s role-targeted structure with ChatGPT’s accurate academic and clinical phrasing.

Future Application and Improvement: I will apply this multi-tool comparison approach whenever drafting professional career documents or healthcare communications to ensure both visual polish and factual precision, and I can further improve my resume by customizing keywords for specific telehealth employers.
