# Activity 7
In this project, I developed and refined a tailored, one-page professional resume for a Nursing Virtual Assistant position by conducting a comparative evaluation of two large language models, ChatGPT and Gemini. To do this, I designed a structured prompt containing my personal details, educational background, clinical skills, and academic projects, along with strict formatting rules emphasizing transferable remote-healthcare skills such as digital documentation, medical terminology, and patient confidentiality. After generating drafts from both AI platforms, I systematically compared their outputs across four key criteria—Structure, Clarity, Formatting, and Overall Quality—and wrote a critical reflection on how each model interpreted the instructions. Using those insights, I selected the stronger role-targeted layout and persuasive framing as my base to produce a polished final resume.
