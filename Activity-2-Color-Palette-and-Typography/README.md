## 📌 Overview

Activity 2 focused on developing the color palette and typography system
for **Luna's Batch**, a bakery-inspired visual identity.

The objective was to create a warm, playful, and recognizable design
that communicates the brand's focus on sweet moments while maintaining
clear visual consistency across its materials.

## 💡 Concept

The concept for Luna's Batch was centered around a **cute, warm, and
playful bakery aesthetic**. The design combines soft colors, rounded
typography, and a friendly illustrated character to create a welcoming
and memorable visual identity.

The overall style was designed to feel youthful and cheerful while still
maintaining enough structure for the brand name and other information to
remain clear and readable.

## 🎨 Color Palette

### Primary Color — #6D99BB

A soft azure blue was selected as the main color of the design. It gives
the visual identity a calm and friendly appearance while providing a
strong background that allows the lighter elements to stand out.

### Secondary Color — #FFE2B7

A light orange tone was used as a warm supporting color. It creates a
soft and inviting appearance and complements the blue background. The
color also reinforces the sweet and cozy feeling associated with a
bakery.

### Accent Color — #5E4B71

A gray-purple tone was selected as the accent color for text,
illustrations, and smaller visual elements. It provides contrast against
the lighter colors while adding depth and character to the overall
palette.

## 🔤 Typography

Three typefaces were selected to establish a playful but organized
typography system:

- **Lilita One** — Used as a display font for prominent titles and
  branding elements. Its bold and rounded appearance gives the design a
  fun and approachable personality.
- **League Spartan** — Used as a secondary display font for headings and
  important information. Its bold and structured appearance creates
  strong visual emphasis.
- **Roboto** — Used as an accent/supporting font. Its clean and simple
  letterforms improve readability and provide contrast against the more
  decorative display fonts.

The typography creates a clear visual hierarchy:

**Brand/Title → Main Heading → Supporting Text**

## 🛠️ Development Process

1. Established a warm and playful bakery-inspired concept.
2. Selected a soft azure blue as the primary color.
3. Added light orange to create warmth and contrast.
4. Introduced gray-purple as an accent color for depth and emphasis.
5. Selected **Lilita One** for the main display elements.
6. Selected **League Spartan** for additional headings and prominent
   information.
7. Selected **Roboto** for supporting text and readability.
8. Tested the colors and typography together to ensure visual
   consistency.
9. Applied the design system to the Luna's Batch branding and layout.

## 🎯 Why These Choices Were Made

The selected colors work together to create a **soft, cheerful, and
welcoming bakery identity**. The combination of azure blue, light
orange, and gray-purple provides enough contrast while maintaining a
cohesive appearance.

The typography complements this style by combining bold, playful
display fonts with the clean and readable Roboto. **Lilita One** and
**League Spartan** give the brand a distinctive personality, while
**Roboto** provides balance and readability.

Together, the color palette, typography, and illustrated elements create
a consistent visual identity for **Luna's Batch** that reflects its
tagline, **"Made for Sweet Moments."**
