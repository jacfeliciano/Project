# Activity 3 — Infographic Design and Information Visualization

## 📌 Overview

Activity 3 focused on creating an **informative infographic about poor time management** and its effects on students. The infographic, titled **"Lost in the Clock: The Impact of Poor Time Management,"** was designed to communicate important information in a clear, engaging, and visually organized way.

The objective was to present the causes, effects, and possible solutions to poor time management while using typography, illustrations, icons, and layout elements to make the information easy to understand.

## 💡 Concept

The concept for the infographic was centered around the idea of **time being difficult to control when it is not managed properly**. The clock and running-person illustrations represent the feeling of being rushed, overwhelmed, or unable to keep up with responsibilities.

The overall design uses a **playful but serious visual style**. Bold typography attracts attention to important information, while illustrations and icons help communicate the topic without relying entirely on written text.

The infographic follows a clear progression:

**Problem → Causes → Evidence → Impact → Solution**

This structure allows viewers to understand the issue first before learning about its effects and the actions that can be taken to address it.

## 🎨 Visual Design

A **soft mint green background** was used throughout the infographic to create a calm and consistent visual appearance. Dark gray-purple text provides strong contrast against the background, while white outlined typography is used for major titles.

Several visual elements were included to support the topic:

- **Clock illustration** — represents time and time awareness.
- **Running person** — represents rushing, pressure, and struggling to keep up with responsibilities.
- **Clock and gear illustrations** — represent problems with managing and organizing time.
- **Calendar, target, and productivity icons** — represent planning, goal setting, scheduling, and productivity.
- **Decorative dashed lines** — separate the different sections and create a clear visual flow.
- **World map background element** — adds visual interest to the research and evidence section.

The combination of these elements makes the infographic more visually engaging while helping viewers quickly recognize the purpose of each section.

## 🔤 Typography

The typography was designed to create a strong hierarchy between titles, headings, and supporting information.

- **Bold decorative display typography** — used for the main title, section headings, and key words. Its block-like appearance gives the infographic a strong and energetic personality.
- **Outlined white typography** — used in the main title to make it stand out from the background and immediately attract attention.
- **Clean supporting text** — used for descriptions, explanations, and source information to maintain readability.
- **Bold text and bullet points** — used to emphasize the major causes and important information.

The typography creates a clear hierarchy:

**Main Title → Section Headings → Key Points → Supporting Information**

This allows viewers to scan the infographic quickly without having to read every section in detail.

## 📝 Information Presented

The infographic explains **poor time management** as the inability to properly plan, prioritize, and organize tasks within the available time. It highlights how this can contribute to procrastination, missed deadlines, increased stress, reduced productivity, poor academic or work performance, and reduced time for rest and personal activities.

### Causes of Poor Time Management

- Procrastination
- Lack of planning
- Poor prioritization
- Social media and other distractions
- Taking on too many responsibilities
- Lack of clear goals
- Underestimating task duration
- Difficulty staying focused
- Lack of motivation
- Poor work-life balance

### Impact of Poor Time Management

The infographic highlights several negative effects:

- **Stress**
- **Burnout**
- **Poor performance**
- **Reduced productivity**

### Possible Solutions

The infographic also presents several strategies that can help improve time management:

- Set goals
- Schedule tasks
- Limit distractions
- Avoid procrastination

## 📊 Research and Evidence

A research statistic was included to support the topic. According to **Devadharshini et al. (2026)**, **51.1% of college students exhibited academic procrastination**, which is closely associated with poor time management.

### Source

Devadharshini, R., et al. (2026). *Study on academic procrastination among college students*. Journal of Clinical and Diagnostic Research, 20(3), IC01-IC04.

## 🛠️ Development Process

1. Selected **poor time management** as the main topic of the infographic.
2. Created the title **"Lost in the Clock: The Impact of Poor Time Management"** to immediately communicate the subject.
3. Organized the information into problem, causes, evidence, impact, and solution sections.
4. Selected a soft mint green background for a consistent visual appearance.
5. Used dark typography and white outlined text to create contrast and hierarchy.
6. Added a clock and running-person illustration to visually represent time pressure.
7. Added icons and illustrations related to planning, productivity, and organization.
8. Used dashed divider lines to separate the different sections.
9. Arranged the causes into two columns to make the information easier to scan.
10. Divided the lower portion into **Impact** and **Solution** sections to create a direct comparison between the problem and possible actions.
11. Added the research source at the bottom to provide proper attribution and support the information presented.
12. Reviewed the overall composition to ensure that the text, illustrations, and spacing worked together effectively.

## 🎯 Why These Choices Were Made

The design choices were made to ensure that the infographic was **informative, visually engaging, and easy to follow**. Since poor time management can be a complex topic, the information was divided into smaller sections instead of presenting it as one large block of text.

The clock and running-person illustrations immediately communicate the idea of **time pressure**, while the calendar, target, and productivity graphics reinforce the importance of planning and organization.

The use of bold display typography helps important information stand out, while the supporting text provides additional explanations without overpowering the main visual elements. The dashed lines also help guide the viewer through the infographic from the problem toward its causes, impacts, and solutions.

Overall, **"Lost in the Clock"** uses typography, color, illustrations, and information hierarchy to turn a serious topic into an accessible and engaging visual presentation. The final design encourages viewers to recognize the negative effects of poor time management and understand practical ways to manage their time more effectively.
